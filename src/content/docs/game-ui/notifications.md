**{ui.gameUI.notices}** are short messages like "You got: Key". They need the [Inventory feature](/novelworks-docs/features/inventory/) to appear for items. The page says so when the feature is off.

## What you can change

- **{ui.gameUIInspector.position}**: top (default), center or bottom.
- **{ui.gameUIInspector.backgroundColor}**: "Same as the dialogue box" by default.
- **{ui.gameUIInspector.textColor}**: "Same as the text color" by default.
- **{ui.gameUIInspector.shownFor}**: how long "You got: …" and "You lost: …" stay on screen, 1 to 10 seconds (default 2.5).

## What they say

- "You got: Key" and "You lost: Key";
- for a stackable item that changes by more than one: "3 × Coin".

One notice is shown for all the items that change together. The same box also shows small system messages, for example "Quick saved".
