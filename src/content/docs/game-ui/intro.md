The **{ui.gameUI.creatorSplash}** shows up to three pictures, one after the other, before the title screen. Use it for your studio logo or a credit. It comes after the fixed "Created with NovelWorks" splash screen, and before the [content warning](/novelworks-docs/game-ui/content-warning/).

## Pictures

Under **{ui.creatorSplash.pictures}**:

- **{ui.creatorSplash.picture}**: pick a picture (any of your images). Clear it to remove the slide.
- **{ui.creatorSplash.duration}**: how long it stays, 1 to 10 seconds (default 2.5).
- **{ui.creatorSplash.addPicture}**: add another, up to three.
- **{ui.gameUIInspector.backgroundColor}**: the color behind the pictures (black by default).

The preview shows the first picture. Press **{ui.topBar.play}** to see them all. A picture whose file is missing is skipped. There is no text option: pictures only.

## What players see

Each picture fades in, stays, then fades out. A click, a tap or any key skips the current picture, once it has been on screen for a moment.
