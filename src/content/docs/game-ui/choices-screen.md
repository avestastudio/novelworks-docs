The **{ui.gameUI.choices}** screen is the look of the buttons of a [choice](/novelworks-docs/branching/choices/). The preview shows a question with three sample options.

## Buttons

- **{ui.gameUIInspector.buttonStyle}**: "{tx.gameUIInspector.sameAsFontsColors}" by default.
- **{ui.gameUIInspector.buttonColor}**: "{tx.gameUIInspector.sameAsTheDialogueBox}" by default (the box color with its opacity).
- **{ui.gameUIInspector.textColor}**: "{tx.gameUIInspector.sameAsTheTextColor}" by default.

The option text comes from your story. Players click an option or press its number key.

A locked option (see [Choices](/novelworks-docs/branching/choices/)) is drawn greyed out and cannot be picked.
