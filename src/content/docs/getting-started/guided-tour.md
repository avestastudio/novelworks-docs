---
title: The guided tour
description: The 14-step tour that explains the editor, and how to replay it.
---

NovelWorks shows a short **guided tour** the first time you open a project. It highlights the main parts of the editor one at a time. Nothing you do during the tour changes your project.

## Start, skip and replay

- It starts by itself **once**, a moment after you open a project. Skipping or finishing it means it will not start by itself again.
- To replay it, open a project and choose **{ui.native.role.help} → {ui.native.menu.replayTutorial}**. It switches the editor to {ui.topBar.story} mode, the Visual view and the Scenes tab first.
- The card has **{ui.tutorial.start}** on the first step, then **{ui.tutorial.next}** and **{ui.tutorial.back}**. **{ui.tutorial.skip}** is on every step except the last, which has **{ui.tutorial.finish}**. The window is shaded during the tour, so you move with these buttons or the keyboard: **Enter** or the right arrow for the next step, the left arrow for the previous one, **Esc** to skip.
- The card shows "Step n of 14". If a part of the editor is not on screen, the card is shown in the middle.
- The tour ends by itself if you close the project or press Play.

## The 14 steps

1. **{ui.tutorial.welcome.title}**: an introduction.
2. **{ui.tutorial.scenes.title}**: your story is made of scenes, like chapters. The left panel holds them, and tabs for pictures, characters and variables.
3. **{ui.tutorial.assets.title}**: import pictures with the **{ui.assetsTab.import}** button or by dropping them on the window, then choose a type for each one.
4. **{ui.tutorial.stage.title}**: the game screen shows what players will see.
5. **{ui.tutorial.background.title}**: choose the moment's background, and keep it for the rest of the scene with **{ui.momentInspector.applyToTheFollowingMoments}**.
6. **{ui.tutorial.character.title}**: put a character on stage and drag it into place.
7. **{ui.tutorial.persist.title}**: characters stay in place until you change them. See [The stage](/novelworks-docs/concepts/stage/).
8. **{ui.tutorial.timeline.title}**: a scene is a series of moments. See [Scenes, moments and the timeline](/novelworks-docs/concepts/scenes-moments-timeline/).
9. **{ui.tutorial.inspector.title}**: everything about the selected item.
10. **{ui.tutorial.choices.title}**: add a choice, make the game remember something, and send the story elsewhere with an "{tx.leftPanel.condition}". See [How a story branches](/novelworks-docs/concepts/how-stories-branch/).
11. **{ui.tutorial.endings.title}**: in the story map, see every ending and what leads to it.
12. **{ui.tutorial.play.title}**: **{ui.topBar.play}** and **{ui.topBar.playFromHere}**.
13. **{ui.tutorial.script.title}**: write a whole scene as text. Both views edit the same story.
14. **{ui.tutorial.done.title}**: you can replay the tour any time from the Help menu.
