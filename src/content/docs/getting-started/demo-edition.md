title: The free demo
description: What the NovelWorks Demo can and cannot do, and how it relates to the full version.
---

**NovelWorks Demo** is the free edition. You get the whole editor and Play mode, so you can build a game and test it from start to finish.

## The limit: exported games have at most 5 scenes

In the demo you can export your game, but **only if it has 5 scenes or fewer**. The scenes counted are the ones listed in the **{ui.leftPanel.scenes}** tab. (The Map workspace's own hidden scene does not count.)

If your project has more, **{ui.native.menu.exportForWeb}** (in the File menu) opens a window titled **{ui.demoExportDialog.exportYourGame}**. It tells you how many scenes the demo allows and how many your project has. It also lists anything else that goes beyond the demo (see below). It offers two buttons:

- **{ui.demoExportDialog.getTheFullVersion}** opens the NovelWorks page where you can get the full version. In the Steam demo, this button is **{ui.demoLimit.wishlist}**.
- **{ui.demoExportDialog.notNow}** closes the window.

Scenes, moments and pictures are unlimited while you edit and play. Delete scenes, or get the full version, to export a longer game.

## What you can add in the demo

The demo opens everything, but it stops you from adding more than this:

| What | The demo allows |
|---|---|
| Characters | 3 |
| Outfits per character | 2 |
| Effects | 10 of the 25: two entrances, two exits, two reactions, two screen effects and two transitions |
| Particles | 2 of the 5: rain and snow |
| Camera shots per moment | 2 |
| Sound files | 5 |
| Variables | 5 |
| Items | 3 |

When you reach a limit, a window says so, with **{ui.demoLimit.continue}** and a button to the full version. Nothing is lost. An effect with a padlock can still be tried in the effects window, but not added to a moment.

A project that already goes beyond these limits (for example one made with the full version) opens normally, and you can keep working on it. It cannot be exported from the demo until it fits.

## What an exported demo game shows

A game exported from the demo is marked:

- the start splash reads **"Made with NovelWorks Demo"** instead of the usual tagline;
- a small **NovelWorks Demo** logo stays in a corner while the game plays.

Your project file itself never changes, and games exported from the full version carry neither mark.

## Demo and full version together

- Both editions use the **same project format**. A project made in the demo opens in the full version without any change, and the other way round.
- The demo installs next to the full version, so you can keep both. It keeps its own settings and its own list of recent projects, and never touches the full version's license. Its window and About window are titled **NovelWorks Demo**.
- To remove the limits and the marks, get the full version and see [Install and activate](/novelworks-docs/getting-started/install-and-activate/).
