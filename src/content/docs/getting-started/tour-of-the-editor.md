## The top bar

- **Back arrow**: returns to your projects.
- **Project name**: click it to open the project settings (game title, author, screen size, features, length).
- **Story / Story map / Game UI**: the three ways to work on your game. **Map** appears here once the [Map feature](/novelworks-docs/features/map/) is on.
- **Undo and Redo** arrows.
- **Save**.
- **Play from here**: plays from the selected moment.
- **Play**: plays from the title screen.

## The left panel

Tabs for **Scenes**, **Assets**, **Characters** and **Variables**. An **Items** tab appears when the Inventory feature is on. The **Features** button at the bottom switches optional parts on and off.

## The center: the game screen

This is the game as players will see it. Click a character to select it and move it, scale it or change its expression. Above it you can choose the moment's background and add characters.

## The bottom: the timeline

The moments of the current scene, like slides. Add moments with the buttons above, then drag cards to reorder them. Use the copy and bin icons to duplicate or delete the selected moment.

## The right: the inspector

The properties of whatever you selected: a moment, a character on stage, a scene, a variable. When nothing is selected, it shows the project settings.
