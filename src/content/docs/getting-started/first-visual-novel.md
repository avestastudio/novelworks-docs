---
title: Make your first visual novel in 15 minutes
description: A step-by-step tutorial, from an empty project to a game you can play.
---

You will make a tiny story with a background, one character, a few lines and a choice. You need three pictures: a background and two expressions of one character (for example happy and surprised).


## 1. Create the project

1. Open NovelWorks. On the project screen, click **New project**.
2. Type a name, choose where it goes with **Browse…**, and click **Create project**. Leave the optional features unticked for now.

Your project is a folder with a `project.json` file and an `assets` folder inside.

## 2. Add your pictures

1. In the left panel, open the **Assets** tab.
2. Drag your pictures into the window, or use the import button.
3. NovelWorks asks what each picture is: a **background**, a **sprite** (a character), and so on. Pick the right category.

## 3. Create a character

1. Open the **Characters** tab and add a character. Give it a name and pick a **Name color**.
2. Add an **expression** for each sprite (for example "Happy" and "Surprised") and choose its picture. The star marks the default expression.

## 4. Write your first scene

1. Open the **Scenes** tab. Select the first scene and rename it by double-clicking it.
2. Under the screen, the **timeline** shows the scene's moments. Click **+ Dialogue** to add a line.
3. Choose a background for the moment, click **+ Character** to put your character on stage, and drag it where you want.
4. Click the dialogue box and type. Press **Enter** to start the next line: it keeps the same stage. Press **Shift+Enter** for a new line in the same box.

## 5. Add a choice

1. Click **Choice** in the timeline.
2. Write the question, then add two options.
3. For each option, pick the scene it leads to. Click **New scene** in the Scenes tab first if you need one.

## 6. Add an ending

Select the last moment of each branch and click **Ending**. Give it a name such as "Good ending".

## 7. Press Play

Click **Play** in the top bar. Click or press Space to advance, press **Esc** to go back to the editor.

To test from the middle of the story, select a moment and click **Play from here**.

## 8. Save

Press **Cmd+S** (Mac) or **Ctrl+S** (Windows). NovelWorks also saves automatically.

## What next?

- Make choices matter with [variables](/novelworks-docs/branching/variables/) and [conditions](/novelworks-docs/branching/conditions/).
- Change how your game looks in the [Game UI editor](/novelworks-docs/game-ui/game-ui-editor/).
- [Export it](/novelworks-docs/publish/web-export/) and share it.
