## Which file do I download?

| Computer | File |
|---|---|
| Mac with Apple Silicon (M1, M2, M3, M4…) | `Novelworks-<version>-mac-arm64.dmg` |
| Mac with Intel | `Novelworks-<version>-mac-x64.dmg` |
| Windows 10 / 11 | `Novelworks-<version>-win-x64.exe` |

Not sure which Mac you have? Open the Apple menu → **About This Mac**. "Chip: Apple M…" means Apple Silicon.

## The first time you open it

NovelWorks is not signed with a paid Apple or Microsoft certificate yet, so your computer shows a warning once. This is expected.

### On a Mac

1. Open the `.dmg` and drag **Novelworks** into **Applications**.
2. Open it from Applications. If macOS says it can't check the app, click **Done**.
3. On macOS 15 Sequoia and newer, open **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll to the message about Novelworks and click **Open Anyway**. On older versions, right-click the app in Applications and choose **Open**.

### On Windows

1. Run the `.exe`.
2. If a blue **Windows protected your PC** window appears, click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
3. Follow the installer.

## Activate NovelWorks (full version)

The full version asks for the **download key** from your itch.io purchase. Without it, you can still open, edit and play your projects, but **File → Export for Web** is locked.

1. Open **Help → Activate NovelWorks…** (it is also on the welcome screen and in **About**).
2. Paste the download key from your itch.io purchase.
3. Click **Activate**.

Good to know:

- One key works on up to **3 computers**.
- It works **offline** once activated.
- To free a computer (for example before you sell it), open the same dialog and click **Deactivate this device**.
- If you hit the 3-computer limit, use **Contact us** in the dialog.
