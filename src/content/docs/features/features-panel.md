---
title: The Features panel
description: Switch optional parts of your game on and off.
---

Some parts of a game aren't needed by everyone, so they are optional. Click **Features** at the bottom of the left panel (or tick them on the **New project** window).

| Feature | What players get | What appears in the editor |
|---|---|---|
| **Inventory** | Collect items and see them in an Inventory menu | An **Items** tab, item moments and effects, pick up / use item zones |
| **Map** | Open a map and pick where to go | A **Map** workspace, **Show map** moments |

Turning a feature off only **hides** it. Nothing you made is lost.
