Turn on **Inventory** in [Features](/novelworks-docs/features/features-panel/). An **Items** tab appears.

## Create an item

An item has a **name**, a **description**, a **picture** (a Prop) and a **Kind**: **Single** (you have it or not) or **Stackable** (you have a number of them).

## Use items

- **Item** moment: give or remove an item, with an optional notice ("You got: Key").
- **Choices**: effects can give or remove items.
- **Conditions**: has, doesn't have, or has at least N.
- **Zones**: pick up and use items.

Players open the **Inventory** with its button or the **I** key. Change how it looks in [Game UI](/novelworks-docs/game-ui/game-ui-editor/).
