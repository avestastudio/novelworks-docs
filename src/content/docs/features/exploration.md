---
title: Exploration (point and click)
description: Let players click around a picture.
---

An **Exploration** moment has no text box. Players click **zones** on the picture, which is great for mystery and escape-room games.

1. Add an **Exploration** moment from the timeline and choose its background.
2. **Drag on the picture** to draw a zone.
3. Select the zone and set what it does in the inspector.

## A zone can

- **Go to a scene**
- **Continue** the story
- **Examine**: show a short line of text, optionally with a speaker
- Change a **variable**
- **Pick up an item** (Inventory feature): the zone then disappears
- **Use an item** (Inventory feature): it only reacts if the player has the item, and can consume it

## Options

- **Label** shown on hover, and a **highlight**: none, glow or outline.
- **Hidden or locked** by a condition. A locked zone shows a message.
- Works **once** or **every time**.
- **Props**: object pictures placed on the picture and linked to a zone.

On a phone, the first tap shows the label and the second tap acts.
