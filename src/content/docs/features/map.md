---
title: Map
description: Let players choose where to go.
---

Turn on **Map** in [Features](/novelworks-docs/features/features-panel/), then click **Edit map**. A **Map** workspace opens.

## Build the map

1. Add a **map background**.
2. Click **Add location** and draw a zone on the picture.
3. Give the location a **name** (for example "Café"). It shows on hover by default.
4. Choose the **scene to play** when the player clicks it. You can create a scene from this field.
5. Click **Test map** to try it.

## When can players open the map?

In the map settings, choose: from the start, after the map has first been opened in the story, or never (the story decides when to show it).

## Show map moments

Add a **Show map** moment in a scene to open the map right then. A map opened by the story asks the player to choose a destination. A map opened with the **Map** button can be closed to keep reading.

## Locations can be locked

A location can be hidden or locked by a condition, with a message when locked.
