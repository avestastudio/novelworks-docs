---
title: Publish on itch.io
description: Upload your game.
---

After exporting, NovelWorks shows the steps. In short:

1. On itch.io, create a new project and set **Kind of project** to **HTML**.
2. Upload the `.zip` and tick **This file will be played in the browser**.
3. Set the viewport to your game's screen size (shown in the dialog), and tick **Mobile friendly** and **Fullscreen button**.
4. Save and publish.
