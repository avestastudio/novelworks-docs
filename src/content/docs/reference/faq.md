## Getting started

**Do I need to know how to code?** No. NovelWorks is built for people who don't: you build the game the way it looks, and press Play.

**Who owns my games?** You do: "Your games belong to you, with no royalties," says the About window. The free fonts included in your games keep their own open licenses.

**Which languages does the editor speak?** English, French, Spanish, Brazilian Portuguese, Simplified Chinese, Russian and Japanese. Change it in **{ui.menu.view} → Language**. Your story stays in the language you write it in.

**Can I use the demo to make a full game?** You can try everything and play it in the editor, but the demo limits some things, such as the number of characters and sounds, and it exports games of 5 scenes or fewer. See [The free demo](/novelworks-docs/getting-started/demo-edition/) and [Licenses and editions](/novelworks-docs/reference/licenses-and-editions/).

## Your work

**Where is my project?** In the folder you chose when you created it. The project settings show it under "{tx.projectSettings.savedIn}". To copy a project, copy the whole folder.

**I lost a change.** Try **{ui.topBar.undo}** (up to 200 steps), then **{ui.native.menu.file} → Restore from Backup…**. See [Saving, backups and undo](/novelworks-docs/getting-started/saving-and-undo/).

**I deleted a picture, a scene or a variable by mistake.** Press **{ui.topBar.undo}**. A deleted picture is kept in `backups/deleted-assets` and comes back with Undo. A deleted scene or variable can be undone, and meanwhile the places that used it show warnings.

**Can I move my project to another computer?** Yes: copy its folder, then open it with **{ui.native.menu.file} → Open Project…**.

## Writing

**Why is a character still on screen at the next moment?** Characters stay on the stage until you change them. See [The stage](/novelworks-docs/concepts/stage/).

**How do I make a choice matter?** Make the game remember something in an option, then use an If… or a condition on an option. See [How a story branches](/novelworks-docs/concepts/how-stories-branch/).

**Can I write my whole story as text?** Yes: [Script mode](/novelworks-docs/writing/script-mode/), and [Import and export a script](/novelworks-docs/writing/import-a-script/).

**Can I add music, sound effects or voices?** Yes: import MP3, OGG or WAV files. See [Sound](/novelworks-docs/writing/sound/).

**Can a background move?** Yes: animated GIF and WebP pictures, and MP4 or WebM videos (looping, with no sound). See [Ambiences and animated backgrounds](/novelworks-docs/writing/ambience-and-animated-backgrounds/).

**Can my characters change clothes?** Yes. See [Outfits](/novelworks-docs/writing/outfits/).

**Can I color or shake some words?** Yes. See [Rich text](/novelworks-docs/writing/rich-text/).

**Can I add credits or chapters?** Yes: [Credits](/novelworks-docs/game-ui/credits/) and [Chapters](/novelworks-docs/game-ui/chapters/).

**Can I use my own font?** Not yet. You can pick one of four fonts. See [Fonts and colors](/novelworks-docs/game-ui/fonts-and-colors/).

## Testing and publishing

**How do I test from the middle?** Select a moment and use **{ui.leftPanel.playFromHere}**. See [Play, Play from here, Test the map](/novelworks-docs/testing/play/).

**What do the warnings mean?** See [Problems: every warning](/novelworks-docs/testing/problems/). A warning never stops you from playing or exporting.

**Do players need to install anything?** No. A web export plays in any browser, on computers and phones. See [Web export](/novelworks-docs/publish/web-export/).

**Where do players' saves go?** In their browser, for your game. They are kept when you update the game. See [Saving and loading](/novelworks-docs/player/save-and-load/).

## Problems

**The export is locked.** In the full version, activate NovelWorks: **{ui.native.role.help} → Activate NovelWorks…**. See [Troubleshooting](/novelworks-docs/reference/troubleshooting/).

**Something else is wrong.** See [Troubleshooting and messages](/novelworks-docs/reference/troubleshooting/), or [report a bug](/novelworks-docs/reference/report-a-bug/).
