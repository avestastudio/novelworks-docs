---
title: FAQ
description: Quick answers.
---

**Do I need to know how to code?** No. NovelWorks is built for people who don't.

**Who owns my games?** You do, completely. There are no royalties.

**Where is my project?** In the folder you chose when you created it. The project name's settings show the path under **Saved in**.

**Can I use the demo to make a full game?** Yes. Only exporting needs the full version.

**I lost a change.** Try **Undo**, then **File → Restore from Backup…**.
