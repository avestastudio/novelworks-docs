title: Report a bug
description: Tell us when something is wrong, and what to include.
---

Choose **Help → {ui.native.menu.reportABug}**. It opens the NovelWorks contact form in your web browser. The same link is in the About window, and on the left of the projects screen.

## What to include

- Your **version of NovelWorks**: it is shown in the About window and at the bottom left of the projects screen.
- What you were doing, step by step, and what you expected.
- What happened instead, and the exact message if there was one.
- If a **warning** is involved, its text (see [Problems](/novelworks-docs/testing/problems/)).
- Your computer: Mac or Windows.

If your project is involved, keep a copy of its folder before you try anything else. See [Saving, backups and undo](/novelworks-docs/getting-started/saving-and-undo/).
