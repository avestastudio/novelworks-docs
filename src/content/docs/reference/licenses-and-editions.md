## Your games are yours

The About window of NovelWorks says: "{tx.aboutDialog.makeVisualNovelsWithoutCodeYourGames}" The fonts included in your games keep their own open licenses (see below).

## Editions

| | Demo | Full version |
|---|---|---|
| The whole editor, Play mode, Script mode, Game UI | yes, with the limits below | yes |
| Export for the web | up to 5 scenes and within the limits below, with a mark in the exported game | any size, no mark |
| Needs a download key | no | yes, to export (itch.io full version). The Steam edition needs none. |

See [The free demo](/novelworks-docs/getting-started/demo-edition/) and [Install and activate](/novelworks-docs/getting-started/install-and-activate/). Both editions read and write the same project format.

## Demo limits

| In the demo | At most |
|---|---|
| Characters | 3 |
| Outfits per character | 2 |
| Camera shots per moment | 2 |
| Sound files | 5 |
| Variables | 5 |
| Items | 3 |
| Effects | 2 in each of the entrance, exit, reaction, screen and transition categories |
| Particles | Rain and snow |

These limits only stop you from adding more. Nothing is lost, and your project goes on as it is in the full version. A project that goes over them still opens in the demo, but the demo cannot export it.

## Activation

The full version asks for the **download key** that comes with your purchase. Without it, you can open, edit and play your projects. Only exporting is locked. The activation window says a key works on up to 3 computers, and can be deactivated to move to another. NovelWorks checks the license on your computer at every start, so exporting also works without internet.

You can see the state of your license in the About window ("{tx.aboutDialog.activated}{tx.aboutDialog.manage}" or "{tx.aboutDialog.notActivated}{tx.aboutDialog.activateNovelWorks}").

## What an exported game includes

- Your game, your pictures, videos, sounds and text.
- The fonts of your Game UI and the dyslexia-friendly font, with their license files. The game fonts (Inter, Lora, Nunito and Courier Prime) and the dyslexia-friendly font are free fonts under their own open licenses (the SIL Open Font License for the four main fonts).
- A short "Made with NovelWorks" screen when the game starts.
- In a game exported from the demo, the "Made with NovelWorks Demo" screen, and a small "NovelWorks Demo" logo in a corner while the game plays.
