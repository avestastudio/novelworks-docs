---
title: Moments and dialogue
description: Moments are the steps of a scene. Learn the kinds and how to write dialogue.
---

A scene is a row of **moments** in the timeline at the bottom, like slides. Each moment has a **stage** (a background and characters) and one kind of content.

## The kinds of moment

| Kind | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Dialogue** | A line of dialogue or narration |
| **Choice** | The player picks an option. It can change variables and lead to another scene |
| **Condition** | Checks something and goes to one scene or another. Players don't see it |
| **Jump** | Goes straight to another scene. Players don't see it |
| **Ending** | Shows the ending's name, then returns to the title screen |
| **Exploration** | No text box: the player clicks zones on the picture |
| **Item** | Gives or removes items (needs the Inventory feature) |
| **Show map** | Opens the map (needs the Map feature) |

## Writing dialogue

1. Select a moment and click the text box on the screen, or use **+ Dialogue**.
2. Choose who speaks in the name tag, or leave it as **Narrator**.
3. Type your line.

Shortcuts: **Enter** goes to the next line (a new moment with the same stage), **Shift+Enter** adds a new line in the same box, and **Cmd/Ctrl+Enter** adds a new dialogue moment even while typing.

## The stage

Each moment remembers its stage. Above the screen:

- Pick the **background**. **Apply to the following moments** copies it to the next ones.
- **+ Character** puts a character on stage.

Click a character on the screen to move it, resize it with the corner handles, flip it, change its expression, or bring it forward or send it backward. **Left**, **Center** and **Right** are one-click positions.

## Notes

Every moment has a private **note** for you. It never appears in the game or in exports.

## Moving between moments

Use the **← →** keys, **Home** and **End**, or click a card.
