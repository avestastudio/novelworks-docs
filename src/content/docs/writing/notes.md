title: Notes
description: Private notes on moments, and summaries on scenes. They are never shown in the game.

## Notes on moments

A **note** is for you only: ideas, to-dos, reminders. Each moment can have one.

- Select the moment and type in **{ui.momentInspector.note}** in the inspector ("{tx.momentInspector.ideasToDosReminders}"). The inspector says that only you see your notes: they are never shown in the game.
- Or right-click the moment and choose **{ui.contextMenus.addNote}** (or **{ui.contextMenus.editNote}**), which moves you to that box.

A moment with a note shows a sticky-note icon on its card; point at it to read the note. The story map shows how many moments of each scene have a note. Notes are found by [Find and replace](/novelworks-docs/writing/search/).

### Notes in Script mode

In [Script mode](/novelworks-docs/writing/script-mode-lines/), a note is a line starting with `//`, placed before the moment it belongs to:

```
// check the timing of this joke
Mia : Hello!
```

A note with no line after it is flagged.

## Notes on scenes

There is no note on a scene, but a scene has a **{ui.sceneInspector.summary}**: a sentence or two for you only, shown on the story map and above the timeline, never in the game. See [Scenes](/novelworks-docs/writing/scenes/).
