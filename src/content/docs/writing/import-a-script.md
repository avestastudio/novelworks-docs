---
title: Import a script
description: Turn a text script into scenes, lines and characters.
---

Already wrote your story in a text file? Use **File → Import Script…**.

## Format

```
# Spring morning
Mia: Hello!
It was raining.
Aoi: Hey… are you okay?
```

- A line starting with **#** starts a new scene.
- **Name: text** is a line of dialogue said by that character.
- A line with no name is said by the **Narrator**.
- Blank lines are ignored.

## Steps

1. Paste your script on the left. The **Preview** shows the scenes, lines and new characters that will be created.
2. Untick names you don't want as characters: they stay in the text, said by the narrator.
3. Choose where the lines go, then import.

If something isn't right, use **Undo** to take the import back.
