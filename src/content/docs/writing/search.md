---
title: Find and replace
description: Search every line, choice and label of your game.
---

Open **Edit → Find and Replace…** (**Cmd+F** / **Ctrl+F**).

Type in **Find in your game**. Results are listed with where they are (moment, option, zone, item). Click one to jump to it.

- **Match case** (Aa) only finds the same capitals.
- **Whole words only** finds "Mia" but not "Miami".
- **Replace with** + **Replace this one** replaces the selected result and goes to the next. **Replace all** asks first, and you can undo it.
