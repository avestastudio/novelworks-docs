---
title: History
description: Re-read what was said.
---

The **History** shows the lines already read, with the speaker in their color, and the choices the player made.

Players open it with the **mouse wheel up**, the **H** key, the **History** button or the pause menu. Close it with **Esc**, **H** or Back. It is read-only, and the story is paused while it is open.

It keeps the last 200 lines and is saved with the game. Change its look in **Game UI → History**.
