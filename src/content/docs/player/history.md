---
title: History
description: The list of lines the player has already read, and how to open it.
---

The **{rt.game.history}** shows what has been said so far: dialogue lines with the speaker's name in their color, the questions of choices, lines shown by exploration zones, and "You chose: …" with the option the player picked. It cannot be used to jump back: it is only for reading.

## Open it, close it

- With the **mouse wheel** (scrolling up on the story), the **H** key, the **{rt.game.history}** button of the pause menu, or the History button of the quick menu if you show it.
- Close it with **Esc**, **H** or the **{rt.game.back}** button.
- It is not available on the title screen, while paused, or while another screen (Save, Options, the inventory) is open. Opening it turns Auto and Skip off.

[Rich text](/novelworks-docs/writing/rich-text/) shows as plain words here, without colors or effects.

## Using it

It opens scrolled to the latest line. Scroll with the wheel, by dragging (on a touch screen), or with the keys: arrows move by a little, **Page Up** / **Page Down** or **Space** by a screen, **Home** and **End** to the ends. When nothing has been said yet, it says "{tx.historyScreen.nothingHasBeenSaidYet}"

## Limits

- It keeps the last **200** entries.
- It is saved with each save and restored on load.
- A new game, or going back to the title screen, clears it.

The look of the screen is set in [History screen](/novelworks-docs/game-ui/history-screen/).
