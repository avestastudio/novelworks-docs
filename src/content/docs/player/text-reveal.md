---
title: Text reveal
description: Lines type in letter by letter.
---

<span class="badge-new">New in 0.7.0</span>

Lines appear letter by letter. A click, **Space** or **Enter** completes the line, and the next one moves on.

- The whole line is laid out from the start, so words never jump to the next line while typing.
- Skip shows lines at once. Auto waits until the line is complete.
- On a choice, the buttons appear after the question is complete.
- If the player's device is set to reduce motion, text shows at once.
