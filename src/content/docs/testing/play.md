---
title: Play and Play from here
description: Test your game without leaving the editor.
---

- **Play** (**Cmd/Ctrl+P**) starts at the title screen.
- **Play from here** (**Cmd/Ctrl+Shift+P**) starts at the selected moment.
- **Esc** returns to the editor.

The Play bar has extra tools: **Restart**, **Show splash** (the "Made with NovelWorks" screen), **Variables & items** to give yourself values and items, and, with the Map feature, a button to open the map now.

What you see in Play is the same engine as an exported game.
