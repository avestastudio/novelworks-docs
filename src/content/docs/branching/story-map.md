---
title: Story map
description: See and plan your whole story as a flowchart.
---

Click **Story map** in the top bar (**Cmd/Ctrl+6**).

Each scene is a card, linked by arrows made from choices, conditions, jumps and zones. The card shows the scene's summary and writing status.

- **Double-click** the empty background to add a scene.
- **Drag** a scene's round handle to another scene to link them.
- **Right-click** scenes and arrows for more actions.
- Use the progress bar and filter to see what is still **To write**.
