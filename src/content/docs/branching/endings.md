---
title: Endings
description: Finish your story, with as many endings as you want.
---

An **Ending** moment shows the ending's name (for example "Good ending"), then returns to the title screen.

You can have many endings. Combine [choices](/novelworks-docs/branching/choices/), [variables](/novelworks-docs/branching/variables/) and [conditions](/novelworks-docs/branching/conditions/) to decide which one players reach.
