---
title: Conditions
description: Send the story down different paths.
---

A **Condition** moment checks something, then goes to one scene if it is true and another if not. Players don't see it.

Example: **Mia affection** **≥ at least** 3 → go to "Good ending path", otherwise "Normal path".

Operators: is, is not, at least, at most, more than, less than.

With the Inventory feature on, you can also check items: **has**, **doesn't have**, or **has at least N**.

Zones in exploration moments can use conditions too, to be hidden or locked.
